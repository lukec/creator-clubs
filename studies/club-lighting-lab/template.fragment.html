What is measured, sourced, and still a model?

Published construction: 515 × 82 mm Henrys Pirouette-derived shape, translucent PE-blend body, polycarbonate shaft, EVA knob, silicone cap, and 32 controlled RGB packages. Flowtoys markets 96 high-brightness LEDs, consistent with counting three emitting dies per RGB package.

Working hypothesis: the 32 packages are shown as 16 opposite-facing pairs because that matches Luke's physical observation; the exact electrical-to-physical index map is not yet measured. Glow and diffusion are perceptual browser fits—not calibrated lumens, plastic scattering coefficients, or a substitute for a real-club test.
