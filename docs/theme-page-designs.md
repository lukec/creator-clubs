# Motion Lab theme studio: circus-act designs

## Status and boundary

This document is the creative brief for browser-only Motion Lab theme pages.
Pages P1-P5 remain the exact 6,062-byte Motion Lab V6 WebAssembly cartridge.
The theme pages below are larger JavaScript sketches: they are deliberately
cheap to browse, compare, and reject before any selected ideas are rewritten
for the club's much smaller runtime.

The designs are informed by the project’s physical observations and rehearsal
feedback, but their audience effect is still an **inference** until viewed on a
real club in motion.

## Circus lens

The central question is not “which palette and which generic animation?” It is
“what tiny impossible event can this glowing object appear to perform?” A page
therefore behaves like a miniature circus act with setup, character, tension,
and payoff. Some effects are intentionally simple icons. Others exploit a
club’s peculiar silhouette, the diffusing body, the sharper handle pixels,
roll, flip, activity, or hand/air classification.

The shared constraints remain:

- the club keeps a colored visibility floor in a dark venue;
- the bulbous body gets broad, legible masses rather than fussy pixel noise;
- the handle can carry smaller beads, sparks, and timing detail;
- white is a punctuation mark, not the default source of brightness;
- sensor causality should be readable when an effect is intended for planned
  manipulation, while musical/show effects may be stranger;
- every page should contain different dramatic ideas rather than the same
  effect matrix in new colors.

## Lightning Storm — seven acts

1. **Sleeping Thunderhead** — a breathing indigo cloud with violet pressure
   bands; the storm is alive before it strikes.
2. **Forked Sky** — irregular white-blue branches appear in different axial
   regions, with a brief violet afterimage instead of blackout.
3. **Sheet Lightning** — broad body illumination arrives as an unpredictable
   single or double flash while the handle keeps a deep-blue horizon.
4. **Ball Lightning** — a bright electric orb ricochets through the club with a
   violet wake.
5. **St. Elmo’s Fire** — cyan corona gathers at both ends while tiny violet
   crawlers travel through the handle.
6. **Thunder Roll** — axial roll winds up the cloud; activity determines how
   frequently it discharges.
7. **Caught Bolt** — held state traps a narrow bolt; airborne state releases a
   whole-body electric fracture.

## Fire / Furnace — six acts

1. **Pilot Light** — a small golden flame breathes above a continuously visible
   ember bed.
2. **Bellows** — motion pushes the furnace from red ember through orange and
   gold to a white-hot body.
3. **Cinder Swarm** — small rising handle sparks enter a broad smoky body glow.
4. **Molten Veins** — slow cellular gold channels move through orange-red
   material.
5. **Fireball Impact** — a hot orb accelerates into the body and produces a
   short, broad collision bloom.
6. **Phoenix** — the club collapses to embers, unfolds into two luminous wings,
   then returns to a steady flame.

## Bioluminescent Ocean — seven acts

1. **Abyssal Current** — deep-blue water carries broad teal currents along the
   body.
2. **Plankton Wake** — roll and flip leave cyan organisms twinkling behind an
   implied wake.
3. **Bubble Column** — expanding cyan bubbles rise from the knob and dissolve
   into white foam in the body.
4. **Jelly Pulse** — the body behaves like a soft luminous bell; trailing
   handle bands suggest tentacles.
5. **Breaking Wave** — a bright crest climbs the club and collapses into foam.
6. **Biolume Touch** — stillness is dark ocean; movement reveals cyan and
   magenta life throughout the shell.
7. **Whale Song** — one very slow whole-club swell for graceful, suspended
   juggling.

## Toxic Reactor — six acts

1. **Warning Stripe** — acid-yellow and violet hazard bands crawl without using
   dead black gaps.
2. **Reactor Heart** — a radioactive core pulses at the thickest part of the
   club.
3. **Contamination Leak** — green material appears to seep from the handle into
   the body.
4. **Geiger Glitter** — sharp yellow-white clicks occur stochastically over a
   green field.
5. **Critical Mass** — pulses accelerate, compress, and release as a brief
   violet-white shock.
6. **Mutation** — roll and flip continuously alter both color and the number of
   broad lobes.

## Arcade Power-Up — seven acts

1. **Insert Coin** — a single gold ping enters the handle and becomes a cyan
   ripple.
2. **Loading Bar** — the club fills from knob to cap in unmistakable stages.
3. **Power-Up Stack** — three successively faster sweeps add energy rather than
   merely repeating.
4. **RGB Glitch** — broad cyan, magenta, and yellow blocks become briefly
   misregistered.
5. **Boss Warning** — magenta alarm fields alternate with thin cyan HUD marks.
6. **Extra Life** — a green-cyan rise ends in a celebratory white star.
7. **Invincibility** — the entire club races through a bright prismatic loop.

## Disco / Mirrorball — six acts

1. **Mirrorball** — many rotating warm-white facets reflect magenta and cyan
   room color.
2. **Four on the Floor** — a simple, unapologetic gold whole-club pulse with
   colored offbeats.
3. **Studio Sweep** — a broad magenta/cyan/gold spotlight scans the silhouette.
4. **Facet Flip** — the flip control turns large reflective planes on and off.
5. **Glitter Drop** — a shower begins in the body and falls into the handle.
6. **Last Dance** — slow warm light remembers earlier cyan and magenta flashes.

## Haunted / Ghost — seven acts

1. **Cold Breath** — pale mist condenses and recedes through a lavender shell.
2. **Apparition** — a broad white figure slowly materializes in the body.
3. **Candle Séance** — a tiny amber handle flame summons a cool ghost above it.
4. **Phantom Trail** — a moving white tracer leaves a long lavender decay.
5. **Possession** — calm pale light snaps sickly green or magenta when moved.
6. **Poltergeist** — broad regions twitch out of order, as if tugged by an
   invisible performer.
7. **Vanishing Act** — the light almost disappears, but a low blue ghost floor
   keeps the prop safe and visible before its return.

## Solar Sunset — six acts

1. **Horizon** — a stable deep-blue, violet, coral, and amber sky that rewards
   the club silhouette itself.
2. **Sunrise** — a golden boundary rises through night and warms the body.
3. **Solar Flare** — a hot arc escapes the sun and sweeps around the club.
4. **Eclipse** — a deep violet disc carries a thin, intensely golden corona.
5. **Sunspots** — broad red-orange cells drift across a gold field.
6. **Afterglow** — sunset slowly loses heat while isolated pink memories remain.

## Matsuri Night — seven acts

1. **Lantern Row** — broad vermilion lanterns breathe over indigo night.
2. **Taiko Heartbeat** — gold and white strikes alternate with deep-indigo
   resonance.
3. **Folding Fan** — roll opens and closes vermilion, white, and gold ribs.
4. **Chrysanthemum Firework** — one axial burst expands and becomes falling gold
   sparks.
5. **Bon Odori** — calm repeating bands bow left and right with the flip angle.
6. **Paper Streamers** — long bright ribbons travel at different speeds.
7. **Festival Finale** — overlapping fireworks fill the large body while the
   handle retains distinct falling sparks.

## Traffic / Racing — six acts

1. **Starting Grid** — three red holds, an amber warning, and a full green
   launch make a tiny story everyone can read.
2. **Pit Lane** — an amber double beacon runs continuously over a red floor.
3. **Checkered Chase** — large ivory and deep-blue blocks race along the club.
4. **Redline** — activity moves the engine from cool cyan idle to orange-red
   rev limit.
5. **Slipstream** — fast white-cyan streaks compress with motion.
6. **Photo Finish** — a finish-line sweep triggers a short whole-club camera
   flash.

## Candy / Unicorn — six acts

1. **Cotton Candy Cloud** — large pink, cyan, and lavender blobs drift through
   each other without becoming gray.
2. **Gumball Machine** — colored round regions bounce inside the body and queue
   through the handle.
3. **Sprinkle Storm** — tiny high-chroma handle sprinkles meet larger body
   confetti.
4. **Taffy Pull** — axial roll stretches two pastel ribbons around each other.
5. **Soda Pop** — bubbles rise and burst into a lemon-white fizz cap.
6. **Sugar Rush** — the page’s deliberately excessive finale accelerates every
   motif into bright pastel chaos.

## Fire and Ice — seven acts

1. **Duel** — one half burns while the other freezes; both remain saturated and
   bright.
2. **Advancing Front** — roll lets fire or ice conquer more of the club.
3. **Steam Collision** — the boundary produces a localized white-cyan plume,
   rather than washing the entire prop white.
4. **Ice Cracks** — sharp white-blue fractures run across a continuously blue
   shell.
5. **Ember Under Ice** — red cores seem trapped beneath a translucent cyan
   surface.
6. **Thermal Flip** — end-over-end pose chooses a whole-club fire or ice
   identity with smooth vertical endpoints.
7. **Aurora Truce** — the rivals resolve into one flowing gold, cyan, and violet
   field.

## What this round is meant to teach

The browser round should reveal which ideas survive simulated diffusion and
movement, which pages feel like coherent acts, and which individual effects
deserve physical calibration. It does **not** prove timing, brightness, sensor
thresholds, or audience legibility on hardware. The next gate is to shortlist
effects in the browser, then port only the winners to a hardware-safe canary.
